InboxRush

Swipe your Gmail inbox clean.

InboxRush is an Android app that turns your Gmail inbox into a stack of cards, so you can get through hundreds of emails in minutes. Sign in with your Google account and decide on each email with a single swipe.

How it works

  • Swipe leftMove the email to Gmail's Trash.
  • Swipe rightKeep it and mark it as read.
  • Swipe downUnsubscribe from the sender and trash the email.
  • UndoChanged your mind? Tap Undo to bring the last email back.

You can choose which inbox categories to work through (Primary, Social, Promotions, Spam) and whether to see only unread mail.

Your email stays yours

InboxRush has no server. The app talks directly to Google's Gmail API from your phone, so your email is never sent to or stored by the developer, never read by a person, and never used for advertising. Deleted emails go to Gmail's Trash, where Gmail keeps them for 30 days. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Free and Premium

InboxRush is free to use, with a short ad break every 25 swipes. InboxRush Premium removes ads for $2.99 a month, billed through Google Play.
